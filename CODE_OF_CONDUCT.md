# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project respectful and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- showing empathy, patience, and respect for differing viewpoints and experience;
- giving and accepting specific, constructive technical feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes, correcting them, and focusing on what benefits the research community;
- respecting research participants, data restrictions, licences, embargoes, and attribution.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, insults, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person’s private information, or knowingly misrepresenting results or provenance.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate these standards. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement decisions should be explained privately where practical and should respect confidentiality.

Report conduct concerns privately to `oliver_paver@yahoo.com`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Anyone handling a report must protect the reporter’s privacy and recuse themselves where a conflict of interest exists. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

## Scope and attribution

This policy applies in project spaces and when a person publicly represents the project or its community. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
